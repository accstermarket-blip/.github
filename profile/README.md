## How it works

1. **Reviewed sellers.** Every shop is approved before it opens, and sellers can earn a Verified badge.
2. **Secure payment.** Pay with crypto (USDT or another coin) on a payment page. No chat-app transfers to strangers.
3. **Instant delivery.** The login details appear on your order page, often right after the payment.
4. **24-hour escrow.** The payment is held for 24 hours, so you can report a problem before the seller is paid.
